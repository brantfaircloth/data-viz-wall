Data viz wall

Four simulations, each built for two monitors: the render page on one, the simulator's own source with the executing line highlighted on the other. All three run continuously whether or not anyone is watching.

Kingman coalescent

Sampled chromosomes traced backward to their common ancestor, with mutations dropped on the finished tree and the site frequency spectrum, π, θW, and Tajima's D filling in at the end.

open →

Gray-Scott coat patterns

Two morphogens on a periodic grid, with the feed and kill rates drifting slowly between six named regimes so the pattern morphs from one animal into the next. Turing's 1952 instability, running live.

open →

Karyotype evolution

An ancestral genome taken apart by inversions, translocations, fusions, and fissions. Nothing is created or destroyed — only shuffled — and you watch contiguity with the ancestor decay block by block.

open →

Hybrid zone

Two populations meet along a transect. Migration mixes their ancestry outward, selection removes the hybrids, and the cline between them settles at a width set by dispersal and the strength of the incompatibility.
